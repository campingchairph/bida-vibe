Game safety

Many party games involve running, jumping, balloons, food or blindfolds. Hosts and players take part at their own risk.

  • Use a safe, clear space with a non-slippery floor, keep breakable things away, and always supervise children.
  • Check for food allergies before food games. Don't put balloons or small items near young children's mouths.
  • Adapt or skip any game that isn't safe for your guests' ages or health.
